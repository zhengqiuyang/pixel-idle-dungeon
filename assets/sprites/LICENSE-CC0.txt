CC0 Public Domain Sprite Attribution
===================================

The 15 sprite PNGs listed below are sourced from Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup's
RLTiles tileset (https://github.com/crawl/crawl/tree/master/crawl-ref/source/rltiles),
which is released into the Public Domain (CC0 / Public Domain):

- Heroes: hero.png, vera.png, irene.png, kai.png
- Mobs: slime.png, bat.png, goblin.png, skeleton.png, mushroom.png, gargoyle.png, imp.png, ghost.png
- Bosses: goblinKing.png, golem.png, dragon.png

(`boneLord.png` and `assets/bg/dungeon_base.png` were custom-generated for Ember Abyss.)
